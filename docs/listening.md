A watch finds the threads worth answering on open sources, ranks them, and says why each one ranks where it does.

## Sources

- **Hacker News**, through its public search: stories and comments, with points, author and time.
- **RSS and Atom feeds**: blogs, newsletters, podcasts and release feeds. Podcast transcripts linked from a feed are matched too.
- **Bluesky** search, and **Mastodon** search and hashtags on the instances you choose. Where an instance limits full-text search, the watch says it covers hashtags only there.

Reddit feeds are not a source.

## A watch

A watch holds its terms (all of, any of, none of, and exact phrases), its sources and its products. A new match arrives once as a finding, linked to where it was found. Posts by your own team never become findings, and you keep a list of blocked authors.

When a source fails, the watch shows the error and its time, and keeps what it found before.

## Ranking with reasons

Findings rank by fit to your products, each with its reasons: the terms it matched, freshness, and whether the thread asks a question you can answer. A finding's own text never changes its rank.

## Answering

Stump has no feature that comments on or votes on Hacker News, so a Hacker News finding goes to a [hand-off](/docs/handoffs) with a brief and facts, and the named person answers in their own words. People who engaged with your accounts first are answered from the [inbox](/docs/inbox).
