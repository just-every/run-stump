Stump works on its own. With Orbit and Vane beside it, a post becomes a contact and a signup you can count.

## Orbit

When a person who can be identified engages with substance on your account, by asking a question, replying with more than a reaction or saying they are evaluating, Stump offers them to Orbit as a contact with the public post they wrote. Stump sends only what is public, and never looks up an email address. Orbit decides whether the person can be contacted.

## Vane

Each published post adds a dated note in Vane, linked to its ledger entry, so changes in traffic line up with what went out. With [tracked links](/docs/links), Vane's attribution shows the signups each post brought.

## Connecting them

An admin connects each one with its address and a shared secret. Stump signs what it sends.
