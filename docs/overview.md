Stump is an earned-channels desk for a small software company. It keeps the communities, newsletters, podcasts and journalists worth showing up in, with each one's rules. It turns your changelog into posts in a real person's own voice, publishes to your own accounts, hands members-only venues to the named person who posts by hand, and records every post in a ledger with its link.

## The daily job

1. Load the [venues](/docs/venues) worth showing up in, with their rules and the sources of those rules.
2. Teach Stump your [products, claims and each person's voice](/docs/brand).
3. Turn a release into posts, a thread, a reply or a pitch in the [composer](/docs/compose).
4. Publish to your own [accounts](/docs/accounts), or [hand off](/docs/handoffs) each post a venue keeps for its members.
5. [Listen](/docs/listening) for threads worth answering, and answer people in the [inbox](/docs/inbox) who engaged with you first.
6. See every post on the [ledger](/docs/ledger), and with Vane, the [signups each one brought](/docs/links).

## What Stump will not do

Stump speaks only as real people and the company. It adds the [disclosure](/docs/disclosure) where a connection to the company needs one, checks every claim against its proof, and holds near-duplicates until they are rewritten. It has no feature for the acts the platforms ban: see [What Stump refuses](/docs/refusals).

## Who it is for

A technical founder or first marketer at a software company of 1 to 50 people, posting from their own and the company's accounts. Roles keep everyone to their part: see [Team and roles](/docs/team).
