A post that mentions the company, its products or a competitor, from someone connected to the company, anywhere but the company's own accounts, says that connection before it moves on.

## How it is said

Stump counts a post as disclosed only when one of these stands in it as a whole sentence of its own. Stump compares the sentence ignoring capital letters and extra spaces. A full stop, "!" or "…" may end it, and "Disclosure:" or "Disclosure -" may open it. Brightwell below stands for the company or any of its products, by its name or one of its other names.

- **A disclosure line, word for word:** the venue's line, or one of your own lines from your team record. The line Stump offers for your connection counts too.
- **I work at Brightwell.** Also "I work for Brightwell" and "I work on Brightwell".
- **I'm a founder of Brightwell.** Also "I am", with "a", "an" or "the", and "at", "of" or "on". The role is one of: founder, co-founder, cofounder, employee, engineer, designer, developer, contractor, consultant, advisor, adviser, investor, owner, CEO or CTO. "I'm one of the founders of Brightwell" counts too.
- **I'm paid by Brightwell.** Also "Brightwell pays me." and "Brightwell paid me.", with nothing after "me".
- **Sponsored by Brightwell.** Also "This post is sponsored by Brightwell." and "Paid partnership with Brightwell."
- **We shipped Brightwell 2.0 with recurring bookings.** Only from a founder or an employee: a sentence that opens "We shipped", "We launched", "We released" or "We built", or "We just shipped", "We just launched" or "We just released", followed by the product's name. Anything may follow the name.
- **The founder is my friend.** Only from someone recorded as a friend. Also "The founders are my friends.", "I'm a friend of the founder." (or "of the founders"), "I'm friends with the founder." (or "the founders"), and "I'm a friend of the Brightwell team." "The founder" may also be "the founder of Brightwell" or "Brightwell's founder", and "co-founder" or "cofounder" counts for "founder".
- **My sister works at Brightwell.** Only from someone recorded as family. Also "works for" or "works on", "My husband is an engineer at Brightwell." with any role from the list above, "My dad founded Brightwell.", "The founder is my sister." (or "The founder of Brightwell is my sister."), and "Someone in my family works at Brightwell." The relative is a wife, husband, partner, spouse, fiancé or fiancée, brother, sister, sibling, son, daughter, child, mother, mom, mum, father, dad, parent, a step- or grand- relative, aunt, uncle, niece, nephew, cousin, or an in-law.

Nothing else counts. A form inside a longer sentence doesn't count ("I work at Brightwell and recommend it"), and neither does a question. A sentence ends at a full stop, "!", "?" or "…", at a blank line, or at the end of the post. A single line break doesn't end it. Other ways of saying it don't count either: "Thanks Ana, founder of Brightwell", "Full disclosure: I'm a big fan", "Disclosure: my friends love it too", "our product", "I'm with Brightwell", "We shipped it" from a contractor or a friend, or "The founder is my friend" from anyone not recorded as a friend. A bio or profile line never counts.

When a post isn't disclosed, it holds, and Stump offers a line to add in one click. That line is the venue's line, else your own, else the plain line for your connection. The line goes first, as a sentence of its own.

A disclosure line says only how a person is connected, such as "I work on Brightwell.", "Brightwell pays me to help with marketing." or "Brightwell sponsors my newsletter." A saved line may add what the tie is for, in a few words after "pays me to", "pays me for", "I'm paid by Brightwell to", "I work on Brightwell as" or "sponsors my". In a post, a saved line counts word for word. Stump won't save a person's or a venue's disclosure line in these cases, and it names the line:

- the line says what a product does without saying how the person is connected, such as "Brightwell sends reminders by text." or "Brightwell pays me to tell you it syncs with Stripe.";
- the line carries a claim that would stop a draft: one that is banned, past its review date or has numbers that differ from its proof, or a number, comparison or guarantee that no claim backs;
- the line uses a word the brand avoids.

The claim check skips only the disclosure sentence itself, and only where it stands as a sentence of its own. A line that runs on from the sentence before it, after "…" or after a full stop and a lower-case word, is checked together with that sentence. Every other word of the post is checked as written.

Where a network hides the end of a post behind "see more", the disclosure must come before the cut.

## When it applies

A post must disclose before it can be marked ready, scheduled, handed off or recorded as posted. Removing the disclosure returns the post to draft.

## Connections

Each person records their connection to the company when they are invited: founder, employee, contractor, investor, advisor, friend or family. Someone outside the company who recommends a product gets talking points and facts only, and says the words are their own.
