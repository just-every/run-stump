The inbox holds the replies to and mentions of your connected X accounts, the company's and your people's, so you answer the people who engaged with you first.

## What arrives

Replies, mentions and quotes, each with its thread, author and link, rendered as text. On X, mentions are read at X's metered price from your [price table](/docs/networks).

## Answering

Reply in public to someone who engaged with your account first. On X, Stump allows one reply per interaction, as X's developer guidelines ask, and an AI-drafted reply on X waits for X's prior approval, so on X write the reply yourself.

Every reply passes the same checks as a post: [disclosure](/docs/disclosure), claims and near-duplicates.

## Orbit

With Orbit connected, a person who asked a question or engaged with substance can go to Orbit as a contact, with the public post they wrote. See [Orbit and Vane](/docs/stack).
