## 0.1.0 — 31 October 2026

Stump 0.1.0 is the first release of an earned-channels marketing desk for a small software company: find where your customers gather, show up in your own voice, and see what it brings in. It runs on your own computer with one command and deploys to your own Cloudflare account, with your own database, network apps and AI provider. A new installation applies its migrations in order; there is nothing earlier to upgrade from.

- **A register of the places worth showing up in.** Subreddits, forums, Slack and Discord communities, newsletters, podcasts, directories, publications and journalists, each with its status, priority, what its readers get, how to post there and what it asks of you. Every rule (who may post, whether AI-written text is allowed, self-promotion, links) keeps the page it came from and the date it was read, and the rules decide the routes Stump offers: where only members may post, the post goes to a member; where AI-written text is banned, the member gets a brief and facts, never generated text. A second venue at the same address is refused, and an excluded venue keeps its reason and an alternative. Members and entry gates say who may post where, and a shared limit across your products holds a post until the venue has room again, naming the date. Import an existing venue list from Markdown or CSV: a dry run first, then apply, a rerun that changes nothing, and Undo.
- **Your products, claims and voice.** Products with their names, competitors, words to use and avoid, and tone. Claims you may make, each with its proof, owner and review date; claims you may not make, with the reason; comparisons that need a dated source; a claim past its review date reads as stale until its owner renews it. Each signed-in person has a voice guide of their own.
- **Changelog to posts.** Point Stump at a changelog entry, a docs page or any address, and it drafts for the accounts and venues you pick through your own AI provider: any OpenAI-compatible address, including a model running on your own computer. The person's voice guide and your current claims go with the request. Each draft links its source and the claims it used, the fields sent and the cost are recorded, and a provider failure leaves no draft and says why.
- **Checks before anything moves on.** Each product claim in a draft is matched to your current claims and their proof, and one with no match is flagged until someone backs it or takes it out. A banned claim stops the post. A missing disclosure of the writer's connection to the company stops it, and on LinkedIn the disclosure must sit above the "see more" cut. A near-duplicate of something already posted or queued is held with a link to its match until it is rewritten, and an image without alt text stops the post. A person and their agent get the same answer.
- **One post, every network.** Write the master post once and Stump opens a variant for each account, counted the way that network counts, with threads split at sentence boundaries. Edit a variant on its own; schedule them all with one press or add them to each account's queue slots. A variant that fails a check holds alone and the rest go ahead. Every save is a revision you can compare and restore.
- **Publishing to your own accounts.** Mastodon and Bluesky (threads, images with alt text, and content warnings and polls on Mastodon), X through your own developer app (each post's charge from your own price table is kept on its ledger entry, and an agent's post shows it on the approval), and LinkedIn member posts through your own app. A post reads as posted only once the network's own id and address are read back; an outcome Stump cannot confirm becomes a hold with an owner and is never retried blindly. Each connected account shows its owner. A network that refuses an account's token marks it expired, holds its posts and names the reconnect step.
- **Hand-offs for places that want a real member.** Where a venue keeps posting for its members, Stump hands the post to the named member with a brief: the exact thread and why it fits, the venue's rules with their sources, the facts with their proof, the disclosure line, and a draft in their voice, or talking points and a box for their own words where AI-written text is banned. The tracked link comes with it. The member posts by hand and pastes the link back; Stump checks it belongs to the venue and, where the network offers an official way to confirm the post and its author, uses it. A held hand-off shows who holds it, why and when it is reviewed, and at that date the lapse action you chose runs: hand it to the next member, move it to the venue's next opening, or let it expire.
- **A ledger of everything that went out.** Each entry holds the address and network id, venue, account, person, "agent for person", the exact text read back, the disclosure line, the claims used, the route and the state. Corrections add a version and keep the original, and a post a moderator removed is marked removed with the reason. Search by text or `utm_content` and filter by venue, network, person, product, campaign, route and state; any filtered view exports as CSV.
- **Tracked links.** Links to your own domains carry source, medium, campaign and content tags, and the content tag names the post or the hand-off, so Vane or any analytics tool counts the visits and signups each post brought. Links to other people's sites are never touched.
- **Listening.** Watches over Hacker News, RSS and Atom feeds (podcast transcripts included), Bluesky search and Mastodon hashtags. New matches arrive once as findings, ranked by fit to your products with the reasons shown, alerted to Slack and a signed webhook, with your own team's posts left out and a company block list of authors. A new watch fills in recent matches, up to 30 days back where the source keeps them. Check now reads a watch's sources at once and says why when nothing is new. A Hacker News finding goes to a hand-off, and the named person answers in their own words.
- **An inbox for the people who engaged first.** On X, mentions and replies arrive with the post they answer, read once at the price in your own price table. Replies go only to people who engaged first, one per interaction, through the same claim, disclosure and near-duplicate checks.
- **Orbit and Vane.** People who engaged with substance are offered to Orbit as contacts with the public post they wrote, signed, with an email only when their own profile shows one. Each published post adds a dated note in Vane with its link, so a change in traffic lines up with what went out, and Vane counts each post's signups by its `utm_content`.
- **Pause.** One press stops every scheduled post, or one account's, with who paused it and when. On resume Stump lists what will go out and what stays held, so nothing posts late by surprise.
- **A team and its agents.** People join by invitation, with a role (owner, admin, editor, contributor, poster or viewer) and their connection to the company. An agent connects through one MCP door, acts for one person with exactly their role under your company's policy, takes an idempotency key on every call, and stops when its credential is revoked.
- **No personas, and nothing the platforms ban.** Stump speaks only as real people and the company. It has no feature for keyword-triggered replies, software-started likes, reposts or comments, vote or review requests, or bought engagement, and asking for one answers with the rule and its source.
- **Bring your Buffer history.** Upload your Buffer profiles with their pending and sent updates, as Buffer's API returns them: channels become accounts waiting to be connected, sent posts fill the ledger with their results, posting schedules become queue slots, and queued posts arrive paused until you confirm Buffer no longer holds them. A rerun changes nothing, and Undo takes the import back.
- **Take it with you.** Export everything the company keeps in one archive and restore it into an empty installation: accounts wait to be reconnected, scheduled posts arrive paused and agent credentials arrive revoked. Erase a journalist or an engager when they ask: their data leaves press contacts, findings, the inbox and offers, pitches keep an anonymised reference, and each company post that names them is asked to be deleted at its network, with the outcome. An entry under legal hold is kept.

## 30 Oct 2026: buyer review round applied

The target contract was revised against a 47-point buyer review (the Hootsuite, Buffer, Typefully and Syften buyer's reading). The changes added channels (Instagram, Facebook Pages, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest and Google Business Profile as Core), a multi-network composer, AI editing, an ideas backlog, a browser extension, preview links, own-post follow-ups, review monitoring and requests, AI-answer visibility, more listening sources and controls, alert ingestion from Syften, Octolens and F5Bot, Common Room, Blotato, Later, Publer and Metricool imports, GitHub and changelog release triggers, beehiiv, Kit, Ghost, Attio, Pipedrive, Zapier and Make, company-defined sign-off, per-account grants, mobile compose, link-in-bio, and Later clauses for boosting and creator partnerships. The first release grew from 23 to 49 published clauses so that no published clause depends on a target clause. Dated research moved out of clause bodies into this file and `docs/networks.md`.

## 30 Oct 2026: research behind the network rules

These notes were in clause bodies and now live here; the clauses state outcomes against `docs/networks.md`.

- **X pricing.** X's pricing page listed $0.015 a post, $0.20 a post that contains a URL, $0.005 a post read, $0.001 an owned read and a 3M monthly read cap. The page carries no date. Secondary sources (roboin.io, medianama.com) date the end of the free tier for new sign-ups to Feb 2026.
- **X developer guidelines** (docs.x.com/developer-guidelines, read directly): automated accounts carry the Automated label and name their operator in the bio; replies only after the other account engaged first, one per interaction; keyword-triggered replies are not allowed; AI-generated replies need X's prior approval; scraping or browser automation means suspension.
- **LinkedIn Share on LinkedIn.** The self-serve product page (`w_member_social`, 150 requests per member a day) read on 30 Oct 2026 was last updated in Dec 2023, so the build re-reads it before release (register R-2).
- **LinkedIn member post analytics.** LinkedIn's Member Post Statistics page (`memberCreatorPostAnalytics`, permission `r_member_postAnalytics`, page dated 11 May 2026) sits in the Community Management API's member analytics; the Community Management overview (dated 31 Mar 2026) lists member post, follower and video statistics under the vetted program and says `r_member_social` stays closed. Stump therefore reads member-post results once the company's app holds Community Management access, and takes person-entered results until then.
- **Reddit.** At least four outlets (WinBuzzer, beri.net, Decrypt, TechRepublic) quoting Reddit's 30 Sep 2026 posts report that new public API access requests stop on 31 Oct 2026, RSS ends on 13 Nov 2026, unregistered apps lose access from 12 Jan 2027, and public API access closes in March 2027. Reddit's own posts and its Responsible Builder Policy page could not be read (403). Meltwater joined Reddit's data partner program on 13 Feb 2026 (third-party report, beri.net). GummySearch closed because it could not get a commercial Reddit licence.
- **Threads.** Meta's Threads overview confirms 250 API posts and 1,000 replies per profile per 24 hours, and that profiles without a role on the app can grant access only after App Review; development-mode own-account use and Business Verification timing are forum-reported.
- **Product Hunt and Show HN.** Product Hunt's launch page (no direct requests for upvotes; company accounts may not be makers) and HN's guidelines and Show HN page (something people can try, not a landing or sign-up page; no generated comments; no vote requests) were read on 30 Oct 2026.
- **FTC.** The 14 Aug 2024 final rule on fake reviews and testimonials (16 CFR Part 465) bans fake and AI-generated reviews and testimonials, undisclosed insider reviews, conditional review incentives and bought social indicators.
